# Machine Gun Kane — story arc and roaming jeep

Status: **firmware built and compiled clean 2026-10-12** (`kane.hpp`; 28% flash,
26% RAM, no warnings). The art
sticker sheet (`data/img/kane.webp`), the five chapters, the **mock mirror** and
the **web client** are in, and the **bots** dodge him and read his story
(2026-10-12, docs/bot-testing.md "Machine Gun Kane"). **No TV or sound yet,
and nothing is flashed or synced** (the board also needs `build_web` for the
new `kane-jeep.js`).

Two halves that need each other:

1. **The arc.** Five encounters, met in order, each one going a layer deeper
   into why an old man drives the wasteland shooting at everyone.
2. **The jeep.** A rusted M151 with a machine gun on the windshield frame. It
   roams the surface and shoots survivors caught in the open. You cannot fight
   it. You can only get out of its way.

The jeep delivers the arc. Chapters 1-3 are left at the camps it breaks each
dawn, and its tyre tracks lead you to them.

## Decided (2026-10-12)

- **Name: Machine Gun Kane.** The wasteland's name for him. His tag just
  says KANE. Not "Kelly": that is a living musician's stage name.
- **He can down you.** He stops firing at a downed survivor.
- **Shared arc.** One world-wide stage, like one-time POIs. One survivor's
  ending removes the jeep for everyone.
- **No fighting back, only escape.** No trap, item or choice disables or kills
  him. So the earlier "take the gun from him" ending is cut. The finale
  endings are the truth, the lie, or running (below).
- **The art is image-generated by the user** from the prompt at the end of
  this doc.
- **The song is tone only.** Game text is original and quotes no lyrics.
- **He never sleeps: he is part zombie** (the user, 2026-10-12). At night he
  sits awake at his fire. That matches the art, where he is wide awake and
  grinning in the camped panel too.
- **Jeep size.** It is 25% smaller than the first cut (0.94 HEX_SZ). The camp
  marker keeps its size.

## Who he is

The 1966 canon ([1966-timeline.md](1966-timeline.md)) already has a hole
shaped like him. In `forest/4` (The Evacuation Special), 412 children are put
on a train to Meridian County on 14 September 1966, and it never arrives.

**Kane was one of them, age 9. He is the only one who got off.** The children
on the train stayed nine. He walked away from the siding, so he aged. Sixty
years on, he is about 69 and still nine inside.

His mother put him on that train to save him. He has spent sixty years reading
it as being sent away. He drives the evacuation escort's M151 jeep. The
machine gun is bolted to the windshield frame, and a cable runs from its
trigger to the steering wheel, so he never has to let go of either.

He asks before he fires. Every time.

**He is part zombie.** Sixty years of driving should have killed him, and
partly did. He doesn't sleep, doesn't eat, and doesn't heal. The skin is
cracked through, and the grin no longer closes. The text never says why. One
reading the arc allows: only half of him got off that train. The half that
stayed is still nine and still waiting. What drives the jeep is the half that
left, and it has been dying slowly ever since without finishing. The
wasteland just calls him dead.

He never sleeps, so the night is not rest. He keeps a fire lit so she can find
it, and he doesn't shoot after dark in case she is the one walking in.

Canon rules still hold. The arc never says what ended the world, and nothing
in it settles the Rift.

### The finale

These are the choices on chapter 5's last node. All of them are ordinary
encounter rolls, with no new choice types.

- **The truth.** You tell him she died at 11:47, in the kitchen, inside the
  ring. This is the high-risk roll, because a failure is a point-blank burst.
  On a success he climbs out and walks into the glass for the first time. The
  jeep stays parked where it is, empty, for good.
- **The lie.** You tell him she is waiting past the Seam, where it's still
  1966. This roll is easier, and it is the mercy. He drives off before dawn,
  and his tracks run into the glass and stop. The jeep is found empty at the
  line.
- **Run.** This is the encounter's escape choice. Nothing changes: you back
  out of the firelight, and he keeps watching the dark. The arc stays at chapter 5, so you can
  try again another night.

Whichever ending happens first ends the arc for the world, writes its own K10
chronicle line, and retires the jeep. A failed truth roll doesn't end the arc.
It hurts, he drives off, and the finale is open again the next night he camps.

The ending leaves him a little ambiguous, the way the canon likes. The truth
ending doesn't promise he finds her. The lie ending doesn't say what is past
the Seam.

### Delivery

A chapter counts as read once you reach its last node, whether you bank there
or walk away. A hazard that ends the encounter leaves the chapter where it was,
the same way `endEncounter(..., restorePoi)` puts a POI back.

- **Chapters 1-3 are his camps.** When he breaks camp at dawn, a camp marker
  stays on the hex he slept on, holding the next chapter. Only one camp is live
  at a time. Survey isn't needed: the camp is visible, and his tyre tracks lead
  there. Stepping onto it starts the chapter server-side, the way
  `trapOnArrival()` starts a trap.
- **Chapter 4 is a fixed site.** When chapter 3 is finished, the server picks a
  Glass Fields hex that touches jeep-drivable ground and marks a ring of
  `tireTrack` around that glass region, so the ring shows up on the map.
  - If the map has no Glass Fields, use a Nuke Crater rim instead. The
    kitchen-in-the-glass text then needs a crater variant, or the chapter
    needs a different fallback (open question).
- **Chapter 5 is his fire, once chapter 4 is done.** You walk into the
  firelight at night.
  - Before chapter 4 you have nothing to tell him. Stepping onto his camp hex
    gets his question, a long look, and nothing else: he turns back to the
    fire.
  - The night is still safe, because he doesn't shoot after dark. That's an
    unlock check plus a call line, with no encounter.
- Whichever of these holds the next chapter wears the quests' yellow hex
  outline, so it reads as a quest (see The web client).

## The jeep

### Movement

- **Speed.** He moves 1 hex per world tick (15 s). In a Settlement he moves
  only every other tick: he slows down there to ask.
  - Halved 2026-10-12 at the user's call (it was 2 per tick, 1 in town).
  - The client's glide between hexes is halved too (`KANE_GLIDE` 0.13, against
    the pawns' 0.26), so a step reads as driving.
- **Drivable terrain:** Open Scrub, Ash Dunes, Broken Urban (streets) and
  Settlement.
  - Not the surface Bunker Entrance and Vent Shaft hexes. Stepping onto a
    hatch drops you into the tunnels after the arrival hooks run, so a camp
    or his fire on one would pull the reader underground mid-scene.
- **Never:** Rust Forest, Marsh, Flooded District, Glass Fields, Ridge,
  Mountain, River Channel, Nuke Crater, or anything underground.
- **Route.** He picks Settlements and Broken Urban as waypoints, because he is
  looking for her. He reuses the caravan's waypoint, stuck-limit and re-route
  logic (`pickCaravanWaypoint()`, `CARAVAN_STUCK_LIMIT`).
- **Night.** He stops at dusk (day clock 0.78, the same point `SS_DUSK`
  fires) and sits awake at a fire until two world ticks after dawn.
  - He never sleeps (part zombie). While camped he doesn't move or fire: he is
    watching the dark for her.
  - The night is the one safe time to cross open ground near him, and the only
    time you can walk up to him.
- **Tracks.** He sets `HexCell.tireTrack` as he drives, using the same bit and
  trail renderer as the caravan.

### Fire

Each world tick, for every connected surface survivor who is not in an
encounter:

1. **Exposed** means within 3 hexes of the jeep, and standing on open ground:
   Open Scrub, Ash Dunes or Marsh.
   - Rust Forest, Broken Urban, Flooded District, Ridge and Mountain are cover.
   - **A Settlement is never fired into** (*never shoot at a door*).
   - **Glass Fields are never fired into** (*never look at the glass*).
   - Underground is out of reach.
   - Cover is the survivor's own hex terrain, not a line-of-sight check. That
     keeps it cheap and easy to read.
2. **The call.** The first tick he sees you exposed, he calls out instead of
   firing: one of his lines, plus the engine and voice on the speaker. **He
   stops the jeep to wait for the answer.** That gives you **one world tick
   (about 15 s) to reach cover or get out of range.**
3. **The burst.** If you are still exposed on the next tick, he fires three
   rounds at 35% each, capped at 2 LL per burst. That's about 1.05 LL on
   average, and a 73% chance of at least one hit.
   - After a burst he drives on, and the next burst needs a new question
     (rule one: *always ask first*). So a survivor who stays in the open gets
     a call and a burst on alternate ticks, at most one burst every 30 s.
   - Spent brass is left on the hex (nice-to-have, not built: 1 scrap into a
     ground pile).
4. **Downed.** At LL 0 he stops firing at that survivor and drives on.
   - The death cause is a new `DC_KANE`, so `bots/causes.py` and the death
     screen need it too.

These numbers are a first guess. Measure them in an arena run
([bot-testing.md](bot-testing.md)) before calling them tuned. The last tension
runs had brushes with danger at about half of their budget, and Kane is a new
source of them.

### Visibility and sound

- **Seeing him.** On the map he is drawn only inside your vision radius.
  Unlike the caravan and Doom, he is not shown map-wide. Hearing him is the
  early warning.
- **Engine.** The engine plays in distance bands on the K10 speaker,
  copying `tickDoomAudio()`'s nearest-player band idiom. Gun bursts are a new
  SFX.
- **Calls.** His lines copy the Doom-taunt idiom: only a line index goes on the
  wire (`EVT_KANE_CALL`), and the wording lives in `KANE_LINES` in
  `game-data.js`. They are unicast to the survivor he is calling out.
- **Voice.** The Speak & Spell voice can't say his line yet: `snd-vocab.h`
  has no "mama", "seen", "have" or "my". That needs new LPC words, or else he
  gets a bare engine and gun with no voice.

### Retirement

After an ending, the jeep is stopped for good. The truth ending leaves it
parked and empty at the camp. The lie ending leaves it empty at the edge of
the glass. It stays on the map as a landmark and never moves or fires again,
until the map is regenerated (`wInit()` resets the whole arc).

## Firmware and wire

Built 2026-10-12 in **`kane.hpp`**. The touch points:
- `.ino`: events, `DC_KANE`, `ENC_POOL_KANE`, `ActiveEncounter.kane`,
  `SAVE_KANE_F`, the forward declarations and the include.
- `world-system.hpp`: `wInit()` calls `kaneInit()`, and the LCD marker
  snapshot.
- `actions_game_loop.hpp`: `tickKane()` after the caravan, and a save when
  the arc or camp moved.
- `survival_state.hpp`: `kaneOnArrival()` after the trap check, surface only.
- `encounter_engine.hpp`: a Kane scene never restores a POI.
- `network-msg-encounter.hpp`: the chapter-read hook in `encRunChoice()`,
  and walking away from a chapter doesn't tick the threat clock.
- `network-persistence.hpp`: `kane.bin`, and Kane scenes are skipped by the
  save's POI put-back.
- `network-sync.hpp`, `network-events.hpp`, `network-msg-player.hpp`
  (`enc_path`), `game-server.hpp` (`/state`), `boot-assets.hpp` (the pool).
- `ecology.hpp`: a burst is an injury, so daisies seed there.
- `ui-screens.hpp` / `ui-scenes.hpp`: the jeep on the LCD map, with a fire
  glow at night.

- **World state.** `MachineGunKane K` holds:
  - `q, r`, the waypoint and the last waypoint, `facing`, `stuckTicks`,
    `breakTicks`, and the mode (0 driving / 1 at his fire / 2 gone)
  - the arc: `stage` (chapters read, 0-5), `ending` (0 / 1 truth / 2 lie),
    `campQ/R`, `siteQ/R`
  - runtime only: `called[]` (asked last tick), `visited[]` (heard his
    question at the fire tonight), `saveWanted`
- **Save: `/save/kane.bin`, not `SaveHeader`.** It is its own file like
  `ground.bin` and `trail.bin`, so there was **no SAVE_VERSION bump** and no
  world reset.
  - A save without the file loads a fresh jeep and an unread arc.
  - The record is bounds-checked and ignored if it is for another board.
  - It is written on every save. Saves also fire when he camps, breaks camp,
    or a chapter is read.
- **Encounter pool.** `index.json` `"kane": {"count": 5, "path": "kane"}`,
  parsed like `"traps"`. `ENC_POOL_KANE = NUM_TERRAIN + 1`; the file is the
  chapter.
  - Scenes open server-side on arrival (`kaneOpen()`). Only one reader is
    allowed per chapter at a time.
  - The move handler pushes
    `enc_path {"biome":"kane","id":n,"kane":n}`.
  - `enc_start` carries `"kane": n`.
- **Chapter read.** Reaching a terminal, non-escape node calls
  `kaneChapterRead()`.
  - Chapters 1-3 clear the camp. Chapter 3 places the site and stamps the
    ring of `tireTrack` round its glass region.
  - Chapter 5 reads `kane_ending` off the node: the mode becomes gone, and on
    the lie the jeep is parked next to the site.
  - Walking away, a hazard that ends the scene, dawn or a disconnect leave
    the chapter unread.
- **Events** (`PROTO_VERSION` 8):
  - `kane_call {pid,q,r,camp,idx}`: unicast. `camp` 0 = on the road, 1 = at
    his fire (before chapter 4: his question and nothing else). `idx` is
    reduced mod the client's line table.
  - `kane_fire {pid,q,r,hits,llLost,ll}`: broadcast. `q/r` is the jeep,
    where the tracers start.
  - `kane_arc {pid,stage,ending}`: broadcast, with a chronicle line per
    chapter and per ending.
  - `downed` / `left` carry the cause `"machine gun kane"`.
- **Wire object.**
  `"kane":{"q","r","m","f","st","e","cq","cr","gq","gr"}`, in sync, in the
  tick broadcast's `world`, and top-level in `/state`.
  - `cq/cr` is the live camp, -1 when there isn't one.
  - `gq/gr` is the house in the glass, sent only while `st == 3`.
  - Everyone gets his position. The client draws it only inside its own
    vision.
- **Content.** Written 2026-10-12. `scripts/gen_kane.py` writes
  `data/encounters/kane/1-5.json`; edit the generator, not the JSON (the Null
  Meridian rule).
  - Top-level `"kane": {"chapter": n}`, and `"kane_ending": "truth" | "lie"`
    on chapter 5's two endings.
  - Run is chapter 5's `escape: true` node, the traps' back-out door.

## The web client

Built 2026-10-12. Kane's code lives in **`data/kane-jeep.js`** (in
`web-assets.json` after `world-entities.js`), and the shared files only get
small hooks:
- `engine.js`: tiles.json `kane` / `kaneCell` go into `tileAtlas`.
- The house in the glass wears landmark tile `6_14` (Glass Fields cell 14,
  2026-10-14) while it is the next thing to find: `kaneTileKey()`, swapped in
  by `questTileKey()` in `engine.js`, and on the TV by `observer.js`. Over the
  tile only the pulse at her door is drawn (`KANE_HOUSE_DOOR`);
  `drawKaneHouse()` is the fallback until the sheet loads.
- `renderer.js`: layers `kane` (after `caravan`, under the pawns), `kane_fx`
  (after `characters`) and `kane_light` (last, over the night tint).
- `network.js`: `kaneApplyWorld()` from `_applyWorldState`, the three `ev`
  cases, and an `enc_start` branch that leaves the hex's POI alone.
- `ui-encounter.js`: `kaneCh` comes from the file's `kane.chapter`, so the
  kicker reads "MACHINE GUN KANE · CHAPTER n OF 5" and walking away says it
  will still be here. It's the same JSON-derived idiom as the tunnel panels'
  `panelMode`.
- `game-data.js`: `KANE_LINES` (16 road calls) and `KANE_CAMP_LINES` (8 at
  his fire), reduced mod length like the Doom's taunts.
- `style.css`: `.toast-kane`, rust and gun-oil, italic, no breathing.

What it draws and does:
- **The jeep** is the sticker cell for his mode, mirrored when `f > 0`, and
  drawn only inside your own `myVisionR`.
  - When driving, the Motorbike's dust comes off his rear tyre (`bikeDust`,
    key `'kane'`, scale 1.3).
  - At his fire, the flame is drawn by `renderKaneLight()`. The fire particle
    system was tried first: it sizes smoke by spread but rises it in fixed
    px, so a campfire-sized anchor made thin dark rods.
- **Heading (the user, 2026-10-12): he turns before he moves.** The client
  works out the heading from where he is going, not from the server's `f`
  (only used for his first frame). Behaviour:
  - He points at the new hex first and only then glides there.
  - When he asks a survivor, he swings the jeep, and the forward-bolted gun,
    onto them.
  - On a burst he turns onto the target, then fires, and holds still until
    the last round lands (`kaneHoldUntil`). The move from the same world tick
    arrives right behind the event and would otherwise turn him away
    mid-burst.
  - It's drawn as the painting's mirror (nose 175 deg, or 5 deg mirrored) plus
    a tilt toward the heading, clamped to 50 deg. A side change flips
    through edge-on over up to 520 ms.
  - Muzzle, tyre and fire points go through the same transform
    (`kaneLocal()`).
- **Ruts behind him, never ahead (the user, 2026-10-12).** The server stamps
  `tireTrack` on the new hex the moment he steps, but the sticker turns first
  and then glides. So:
  - `kaneApplyWorld()` records each step he hasn't finished driving in
    `kaneTrackMoves`, and clears them once the sticker is there.
  - `TrailField.drawHex` asks `kaneTrackCut(q, r)` and lays the caravan-kind
    ruts of those steps only up to the arc-length point nearest the jeep. That
    is the segment that leaves hex A by the step's side, or enters hex B by
    it; older passes on those hexes are drawn in full.
  - The rng is drawn exactly as for the full rut, so the laid part doesn't
    shimmer as it grows. The cut is quantised to 1/32 hex for the geometry
    cache: about 2 ms per rebuilt hex, two hexes, only while he moves.
  - Checked: the rut never reaches more than 0.25 hex past the jeep's centre,
    which is the ruts' sideways spread on a curve. His half-length is 0.47, so
    it is always under him or behind him.
- **His camp** (cell 3) and **the house in the glass** are drawn once their
  hex is on your map. The house is a code-drawn glass-green doorway with her
  in it, hand up.
- **Quest outline (the user, 2026-10-12).** Wherever the next chapter waits,
  that hex gets the quests' pulsing yellow outline. `kaneQuestAt(q, r)` hooks
  into renderer.js `isPOIRenderable`, so it follows the same sight/survey rule
  as a POI. The hexes:
  - the live camp (`cq cr`, chapters 1-3)
  - the house in the glass (`gq gr`, chapter 4)
  - him at his fire once chapter 4 is read (`st 4`, `m 1`, chapter 5)
  - none after an ending, none underground
- **`kane_call`** shows the line as a `toast-kane`, plus a log line and a hint
  naming the cover.
  - On the road it also draws a "?" over the jeep for the 15 s. The dashed
    range hexagon that used to go with it was removed by the user
    (2026-10-12), and there is no countdown, by choice.
- **`kane_fire`** draws three tracers 110 ms apart from the muzzle (measured
  off the cell: (-0.25, -0.21) HEX_SZ, mirrored) to the target.
  - It also draws a muzzle flash per round, a spent case arcing out, a red
    spark on a hit or a dirt spray on a miss, and updates LL.
- **`kane_arc`** logs each chapter. The truth and the lie get a banner
  ("THE JEEP IS QUIET" / "THE JEEP WENT WEST").
